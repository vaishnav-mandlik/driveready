## What users hate (recurring in reviews)
1. **Paywalls on questions/modes** (blocked mid-level, capped free questions).
2. **Ads** every ~7–8 questions, flashing ads during tests.
3. **Outdated or wrong questions**, answers marked wrong when right, questions that don't match the real test.
4. **Repetitive questions** / small banks; **coverage gaps** ("real test had questions the app never covered").
5. **Sign questions without images.**
6. **Privacy**: tracking identifiers in teen-focused apps.

## Ideas worth adding next (prioritized)
1. **Spanish** content + UI (many states test in Spanish; competitors offer it) — large content effort.
2. **Scenario illustrations** in lessons/questions (intersections, right-of-way diagrams) like Zutobi's real-world pictures.
3. **Share cheat sheet** as text/PDF (Genie paywalls PDFs) — quick win (done: share as text).
4. **Friend challenge** without a backend: share a Daily-Challenge score card.
5. **On-device "explain more"** (no network) — e.g. link each question to the exact lesson section.

DriveReady Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Applies to the DriveReady app for Android (and iOS when released)

The short version

DriveReady does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The released Android app does not even request permission to access the internet.

Information we collect

None. We do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves your study data only on your device: your selected state and test type, settings, answers and progress statistics, saved questions, exam history, achievements and (if you use it) your supervised driving log. You can erase it at any time in Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

If you have Android Backup turned on, Android may include this on-device data in your personal device backup (for example, to restore your progress on a new phone). That backup is managed by your device and your Google account under Google’s terms; we never have access to it.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — only if you turn on the daily study reminder. You can turn it off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup (receive boot completed) — only used to re-schedule your study reminder after your phone restarts.

Read-aloud uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine.

Children

DriveReady is designed for anyone studying for a driver knowledge test, including teenagers. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Links to official websites

The app links to official government handbooks and websites so you can verify information. Those sites have their own privacy policies. DriveReady is not affiliated with any government agency.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
